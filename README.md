# Pradeepraj Digital Portfolio — Final Experience

This version keeps the latest font-size/layout adjustments and introduces the final unified full-screen visual treatment.

## Intro video
Place your talking-person video at:

`public/intro.mp4`

The landing screen stays paused until the visitor clicks **START EXPERIENCE**. The video then plays from start to finish; the next chapter begins from the video's `ended` event, so a 16-second video is not cut short.

## Background music
If used, place the background track at:

`public/audio/background.mp3`

Browser audio policies mean sound is unlocked by the visitor's Start Experience click.

## Visual direction
- One continuous full-screen stage, not a visible left/right split.
- Presenter/video blends into the right side with a soft feathered edge.
- Left story content floats over the same atmospheric background.
- Subtle drifting blue/cyan/violet light dots.
- No hard presenter card, frame, label, or vertical divider.
- Existing career/education/achievement content remains intact.
