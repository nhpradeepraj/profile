6. SOUND BEHAVIOR
==================================================

There are TWO independent audio sources:

1. Intro video audio
2. Portfolio background music

They MUST NOT interfere with each other.

CRITICAL AUDIO LOGIC:

When the intro video is playing:

- Intro video audio plays normally.
- Background music MUST remain paused.

When the intro video ends:

- Background music starts automatically.
- Portfolio continues to the next chapter.

The SOUND ON/OFF button controls ONLY background music.

It must NEVER mute or unmute the intro video's audio.

If the visitor clicks SOUND OFF during the intro:

- Intro video continues normally with its own audio.
- Background music remains disabled.

If the visitor clicks SOUND ON during the intro:

- Do NOT start background music while the intro video is still playing.
- Remember the user's preference.
- Start background music only after the intro finishes.

After the intro:

SOUND ON:
- background.mp3 plays.

SOUND OFF:
- background.mp3 pauses.

The music should loop continuously.

Use a low comfortable volume around:

0.12–0.18

Do not make the background music overpowering.
